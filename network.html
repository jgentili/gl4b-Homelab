VLANs & Addressing Plan

Standardized IP allocation scheme across six subnets. On every VLAN, .1 is the OPNsense gateway, .2–.49 are static/reserved infrastructure assignments, and .100–.250 form the DHCP pool.

Switch Tagging Note: The TP-Link TL-SG108E managed switch can only manage itself on untagged VLAN 1. Consequently, Management (VLAN 20) travels untagged on the wire as switch VLAN 1, while VLANs 10, 30, 40, 50, and 60 are 802.1Q tagged.

IP Allocation Pattern

10.0.x.1 — Gateway

OPNsense virtual interface on each VLAN providing stateful routing, DHCP, and NTP services.

10.0.x.2 – .49 — Static

Reserved static assignments for hypervisor hosts, switches, APs, LXCs, and Docker VMs.

10.0.x.100 – .250 — DHCP

Dynamic client pools managed by OPNsense, handing out 10.0.10.2 & 10.0.10.3 for DNS and home.gl4b.xyz search domain.
